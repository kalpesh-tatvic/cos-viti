Decide · Exception Handling

Decision · Rate card exception
11:32 AM · Detected Setup fee reduction requested in email thread (AE → CFO, CEO cc'd). Rate card floor triggered. Routed to exception process.
12:20 PM · Tracked CFO asked for specifics. AE replied with details: standard pricing on related service, 1-year contract, fee reduced as urgency incentive. All exchanges tracked automatically.
12:30 PM · Logged CFO approved. Decision recorded with reasoning. Precedent logged: CFO can approve rate card exceptions when paired with a full-rate service contract.
What Viti did: Read an email thread, identified the rate card violation, routed it to the right decision-maker with context, tracked the back-and-forth, and logged the precedent. The CEO had visibility without a single task.
Without Viti This sits in the CEO's inbox for 2–3 days. The CFO never sees it until someone forwards it. The precedent is never recorded — six months later, the same question gets asked from scratch.
